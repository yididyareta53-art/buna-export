01 — Origin

Coffee began here.
Every cup carries that.

Legend places the discovery of coffee in the forests of Kaffa, Ethiopia, where wild Coffea arabica still grows in the shade of native trees. We work directly with smallholder farmers across Yirgacheffe, Sidama, and Guji to export lots that speak plainly of where they're from — nothing standardized, nothing smoothed over.
